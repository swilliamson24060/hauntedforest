## Location-specific sculptures
The 23 GLBs listed in assets/models/manifest.json were generated for Hollowmere. Robed bodies derive from the user-supplied Wraith of the Void sculpt; additional geometry, creature bodies, scene-node articulation, and spectral materials were created for this project. Derivative models retain the underlying source asset's provenance. Optimization uses meshoptimizer 0.24.0 (MIT), a development dependency only.
